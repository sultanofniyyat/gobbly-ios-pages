Gobbly.io – Support

iPhone app · Sultan of Niyyat

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email us at sultanofniyyat@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within two working days. Telling us your iPhone model and the level you were on helps.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to roll Gobbly. Eat the goal items shown at the top before the timer runs out. Every bite makes you bigger, so you can eat bigger things. Watch out for bombs!

A purchase did not arrive

In the game, open Settings and tap RESTORE. This asks the App Store to send again anything you paid for that the game did not receive. If it still does not arrive, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress

Your progress is saved on your iPhone only. It is not stored online, so deleting the app or moving to a new iPhone starts the game again.

Sound and vibration

You can turn music, sound effects and vibration on or off in the game's Settings.

Privacy

Gobbly.io collects no personal information. Read the privacy policy.
